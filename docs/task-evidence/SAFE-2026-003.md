## Scope Implemented

Courier safety reporting validates order-leg ownership, records a separate safety event, preserves the authoritative order, routes active-job support to operations and redacts sensitive references from ordinary viewers. Secure photo capture is available. Admin safety order actions are explicit, TOTP/idempotency protected and state-safe; courier lifecycle transitions are blocked while an order leg is under safety control. The courier client persists a minimal Room incident draft and replays it with the original idempotency key after reconnect; photos are intentionally not queued.
