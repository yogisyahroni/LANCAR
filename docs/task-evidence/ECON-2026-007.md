## Scope Implemented

- The existing server-side food quote contract exposes subtotal, delivery fee, platform fee, tax, discount, total, market, pricing rule version, and structured pricing components before order creation; food order creation revalidates the quote server-side.
- Centralized cancellation eligibility into a versioned policy decision. Food policy distinguishes pre-cost states, searching with/without an assigned courier, active accepted/picking-up states, and post-pickup states; the pricing snapshot market is captured in the policy version.
- Refund records now persist policy version, cancellation fee amount, and a JSON fee breakdown containing paid amount, refund amount, service fee, original state, courier assignment, market, and reason. The customer cancellation endpoint waits for the authoritative refund calculation and returns that breakdown.
- Added a protected Admin customer-fee-credit endpoint. It validates the customer/order/refund relationship and charged fee, updates the authoritative customer wallet, appends a `customer_fee_credit` ledger entry, and writes an `audit_logs` row. It never overwrites `orders.total_price_idr` or refund history.
