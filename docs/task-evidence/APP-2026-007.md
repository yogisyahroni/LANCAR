## Acceptance Criteria Source

Original checklist from `task-food-marketplace-parity-2026.md`:

- `[x]` Flags support `off`, `on`, percentage rollout, market/city, app version and cohort conditions.
- `[x]` Emergency kill switch can hide/disable non-safe entry point without waiting for store release.
- `[x]` Transaction already in progress is not abandoned because its entry flag turns off; active-order recovery remains accessible.
- `[x]` Flag evaluation revision is logged for debugging/experiments.
- `[x]` Financial/security invariant cannot be disabled by a marketing feature flag.
- `[x]` High-blast-radius flags require elevated approval and rollback plan.

These are the original APP-2026-007 requirements. Staging publication, provider cutover and interactive device validation are release follow-ups because they are not additional checklist requirements in this TASK-ID.

## Scope Implemented

- Added a server-owned evaluator for `off`, `on` and stable SHA-256 actor percentage rollout. It supports client type, market, city, app-version range, cohort and required-capability conditions, with fail-closed behavior for invalid rollout or anonymous partial rollout.
- Added per-request public flag resolution and compatibility-aware cache handling. Public responses carry `evaluation_revision`; targeting headers are included in `Vary`, and raw rows rather than another actor's resolved result are cached.
- Added three safe entry flags for customer food, merchant menu and courier service discovery. Their kill-switch behavior only removes the optional entry surface. Customer active-order routes, courier active offers/proof/chat/recovery flows, and merchant order dashboard routes remain available.
- Added server revision persistence, immutable flag-change history fields and structured `feature_flag_evaluation` logging. Every toggle/config change advances the revision and invalidates the public cache.
- Protected payment, pricing, authorization, routing, risk, security, settlement and system controls are not exposed through the marketing/public flag response. High-blast-radius changes require `super_admin` or `ops_security` plus a rollback plan of at least 20 characters; the existing admin auth/TOTP gates remain in place.
- Wired the evaluator contract into Customer Android, Merchant Android, Courier Android and Customer Web. Admin Feature Flags UI now collects the rollback plan and prevents submission of incomplete high-blast changes.
