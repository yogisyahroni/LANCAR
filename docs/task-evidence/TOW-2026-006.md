## Scope Implemented

- Towing tracking chooses pickup or destination as the active route target based on the authoritative stage and returns a towing-specific timeline alongside route/ETA data.
- Customer Android renders the towing timeline with human-readable stages and keeps the existing polling, socket-triggered refresh, last-known route, and stale-data messaging behavior.

- Existing location quality filters remain in force; invalid, spoofed, or stale courier locations are not exposed as the customer’s live location.
