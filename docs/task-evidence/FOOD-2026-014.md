## Scope Implemented

- `ListFoodMerchantsWithOptions` is the production discovery path behind the authenticated food merchant endpoint.
- Server-owned filters cover open/approved/paused eligibility, halal status, merchant/address/menu search, and menu-item cuisine category.
- Server-owned sort modes are explicitly allowlisted: distance, rating, popular, recent, and favorites. Popularity is derived from non-cancelled/rejected food orders; favorites and recent ordering are customer-scoped from persisted data.
- Pagination is server-owned with `LIMIT/OFFSET`, default limit 50, maximum 100, and non-negative offset handling.
- Sponsored eligibility is a separately labelled lateral signal (`AdLabel: Sponsored`) and is not used as an organic ranking input.
- Customer Android browse requests and exposes the sort modes through the existing TEMBUS food flow without changing the OTP/payment boundary.
