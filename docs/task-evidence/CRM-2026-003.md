## Scope Implemented

CRM membership plan/entitlement schema is versioned by market, currency and billing cycle, with a benefit catalog. An authenticated internal eligibility endpoint checks entitlement state, period, market, service and benefit configuration and returns the server-authoritative funding source/cap. The Food order service uses its canonical membership repository and the same server-side benefit policy in both quote and order creation, so an eligible benefit cannot be client-composed or applied outside the entitlement period. The Food owner also has provider-driven pending/active/grace/expired/cancelled/refunded transitions, renewal period extension, an append-only payment-event bridge, and an authoritative subsidy ledger; failed pending payment cannot become active and refunded entitlements are not eligible. Future non-Food consumers must use the internal CRM eligibility boundary rather than adding a second source of truth.
