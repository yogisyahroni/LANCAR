## Scope Implemented

- Added the market policy registry with country, currency/minor-unit, timezone, locale, vehicle/document/tax/payout requirements, policy version, active state, and cross-border policy.
- Added per-courier market verification, market-change request, and explicit deny-by-default cross-border eligibility records. Direct profile market changes are rejected unless the courier is offline and an approved request plus current target verification exists.
- Wired market eligibility into capability/offer eligibility, duty-online enforcement, profile response, courier application resubmission, and mobile market-eligibility APIs. Admin market changes invalidate approved verifications through `reverification_required`.
- Added native minor-unit and immutable localization snapshot handling for ledger/payout rows. Historical ledger facts remain append-only; new USD probe rows were localized with `amount_minor`, currency, timezone, and locale. IDR writers remain backward compatible.
- Added localized earnings/payout response fields and Android rendering with formatted currency/local time, plus an in-app eligibility warning.
- Added an admin dashboard surface for creating/updating market policy and approving/rejecting market-change requests.
