## Scope Implemented

- Customer towing booking now requires GPS pickup and a geocoded destination with non-zero coordinates before quote or order creation.
- Vehicle type, make, model, condition, damage, access constraints, and notes are sent as structured `package_details.vehicle_details` data.
- Towing requests no longer send parcel-only `small`, zero dimensions, zero weight, or fake receiver/phone values. The destination contact is collected and validated.
- The booking review displays pickup, destination, route distance, and route operator/provider from the server price breakdown.
- The customer order endpoint rejects towing orders without the required structured vehicle facts and destination contact.
