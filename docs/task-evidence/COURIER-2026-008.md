# Evidence — COURIER-2026-008

## Acceptance Criteria Source

Original requirements from `task-food-marketplace-parity-2026.md`:

- `[ ] Metric definition/window visible to courier where used for enforcement.`
- `[ ] One anomalous rating does not automatically cause opaque punishment.`
- `[ ] Quality score versioned and appealable for material decisions.`
- `[ ] Service-specific metrics separated where needed.`

## Scope Implemented

- Added the versioned courier quality scorecard policy with explicit definitions, 30-day/90-day windows, units, enforcement scope and courier visibility.
- Reused the existing courier performance source tables: `order_legs`/`orders` for completion and SLA, `courier_proof_attempts` for proof quality, `driver_penalty_log` for recorded preventable cancellations, `courier_ratings` for customer rating, and reviewed `courier_safety_events` for the review-only incident signal.
- Added robust rating handling: one material outlier is surfaced as a review signal, excluded from the enforcement score, and never becomes an automatic opaque penalty. The raw average remains visible.
- Added service-separated 30-day metrics, including food delivery, and rendered scorecard definitions/windows and per-service metrics in the courier profile.
- Added an idempotent mobile appeal route and persisted appeal storage. Server-side version validation prevents a client from submitting a fabricated score. Admin list/review routes persist final decisions and audit events.
