# Cost and unit economics — PART AG

The canonical cost record is per market, service, order, provider attempt,
communication delivery, storage/GB-month, compute-hour, and support case.
Provider invoices and internal usage events reconcile by period, currency,
timezone, and immutable reference. Campaign funding distinguishes merchant,
platform, Ads, and customer promo; loyalty points are liability, not cash.

Dashboards must show actual-versus-budget, anomaly threshold, missing invoice,
and unmatched usage. Corrections use a reasoned compensating record and never
rewrite provider statements. The finance owner signs off unit economics before
market expansion or a large campaign.
