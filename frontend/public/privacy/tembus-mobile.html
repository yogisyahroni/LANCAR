TEMBUS Mobile Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TEMBUS handles data in the TEMBUS Courier and TEMBUS Customer mobile applications. TEMBUS uses this data to provide logistics, order tracking, dispatch, payment or payout status, notification, safety, support, and reliability features.

This page covers the mobile applications with package names com.tembus.courier and com.tembus.customer.

Data We Collect

Data type Examples Purpose
Account and contact information Name, phone number, email address, account identifiers Authentication, account management, support, and order coordination
Order and delivery information Pickup and delivery addresses, order status, delivery proof, recipient notes Order creation, routing, fulfillment, dispute handling, and support
Location information Approximate and precise location, route and tracking updates Courier dispatch, navigation, order tracking, address selection, and safety
Payment and payout metadata Payment status, transaction references, courier payout request data, bank payout details when provided Checkout, payout processing, accounting, fraud prevention, and dispute support
Photos and files Proof of delivery images, upload evidence, support attachments Delivery verification, issue resolution, and support
Device and app information Device identifiers, app version, notification token, crash logs, diagnostic events Push notifications, security, fraud prevention, app diagnostics, and reliability

How We Use Data

  • To create and manage user, courier, and customer accounts.
  • To authenticate users and support OTP or other account security flows.
  • To create, dispatch, route, track, and complete orders.
  • To send operational notifications about orders, account activity, and delivery updates.
  • To process payment status, payout status, refunds, disputes, and accounting records.
  • To protect users, detect abuse, investigate issues, and improve app reliability.

Service Providers

TEMBUS may use service providers to operate the apps, including backend hosting, Firebase or Google services for notifications and diagnostics, map providers for routing and maps, and payment providers for checkout or payout status. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their services to TEMBUS and its users.

Location Data

The courier app uses location data for dispatch, routing, live order tracking, and safety. The customer app may use location data for address selection, order tracking, and delivery support. Background location is used only where required for active delivery or tracking functionality.

Data Security

TEMBUS uses HTTPS for data in transit in production builds and applies access controls to backend systems. Mobile apps store sensitive session data using encrypted storage where supported by the platform. Users should keep their device operating system updated and protect access to their device.

Data Retention

TEMBUS keeps data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain audit records. Operational data such as device tokens or local session data may be removed when a user logs out, deletes an account, or when the data is no longer needed.

Data Deletion Requests

Users may request account or personal data deletion by contacting TEMBUS support. TEMBUS may need to verify the requester's identity and may retain records required for legal, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute, or safety obligations.

Children

The TEMBUS Courier and TEMBUS Customer apps are not directed to children. Users who believe a child has provided personal data should contact TEMBUS support so the issue can be reviewed.

Changes To This Policy

TEMBUS may update this policy when the apps, legal requirements, or operational practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact TEMBUS support through the official support channel used by the app or through the privacy contact listed in the public store listing.
